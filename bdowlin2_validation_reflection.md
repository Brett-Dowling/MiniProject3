# Validation Reflection — bdowlin2

I reviewed Jacob Miller's (jmill291) ten projects across five fields. Before discussion, we agreed on 39 of 50 comparisons (78%). We resolved the eleven differences by keeping ten validator values and using one compromise, bringing agreement to 100%. The compromise for FreeSurfer was “N/A: no observed inactivity gap,” which clarified that continuous activity does not require a recovery label.

Maintenance and release preparation often appeared before long gaps, as seen in vcfanno and textreuse, although these patterns do not prove why development paused. Recovery took different forms: vcfanno resumed with targeted bug fixes, mapedit received new geometry and editing features, and textreuse's renewed maintenance coincided with a maintainer change. These examples show that a long pause can reflect intermittent maintenance rather than permanent abandonment. NeuroElectro also showed why resumed repository activity does not necessarily mean a working service: its documentation updates described a website outage rather than a confirmed restoration.

One recurring difficulty was distinguishing cyclical activity from irregular bursts. We agreed that FreeSurfer and RItools were irregular because their histories did not establish predictable cycles. Open Force Field was classified as declining based on its sustained reduction in commits from 2021 through 2024. Another issue was the observation window: Falcor and textreuse were adjudicated Inactive using the upstream default-branch history and the September 30, 2025 cutoff. Later commits could not establish activity during the required six-month window.

RecentThemes also required consistent commit samples and attention to overlapping categories. A dependency update can fix a bug or security problem, so different wording does not always indicate a substantive disagreement. We accepted those overlaps where supported and resolved the remaining theme differences using the reviewed evidence. This process showed me the importance of specifying dates, branches, and category definitions before comparing labels. Agreement became more meaningful when the final values reflected a shared interpretation of the evidence.
